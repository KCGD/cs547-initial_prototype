# Semester Planner Tasks

1. **Add commitments.** Enter exams or meetings, mark them fixed or flexible, and set an optional time and repeat frequency for meetings. Queue multiple exams and save them together.
2. **Review all commitments.** See exam dates and meeting days and repeat frequencies in one list. Switch any commitment between fixed and flexible.
3. **Scan the semester.** Compare weekly workloads at a glance and use the heavy-week banner to jump to a busy week.
4. **Inspect a week.** See its date range, workload rating, exam and meeting counts, and commitments in date order.
5. **Preview suggested moves.** See where flexible commitments could move and compare the projected workload in both the original and destination weeks.
6. **Reschedule commitments.** Apply one suggestion, dismiss it with **Keep**, pick a different date, or apply all remaining suggestions together.
7. **Override a week’s rating.** Choose **Light**, **Medium**, or **Heavy** to replace the calculated rating across the schedule, week detail, and suggested moves.
